NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into as of February 1, 2026 between Kingsford Capital Partners, LLC ("Discloser") and the Recipient identified below ("Recipient").

1. DEFINITION

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient, in whatever form, including without limitation business plans, financial data, customer information, trade secrets, methodologies, processes, and any other information marked or treated as confidential.

2. OBLIGATIONS

Discloser shall provide Confidential Information to Recipient solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential investment relationship. Recipient shall hold all such Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party. Recipient shall use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose set forth herein and for no other purpose whatsoever.

3. RECIPIENT REPRESENTATIONS

Recipient represents and warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement and that it will not, directly or indirectly, use any Confidential Information to compete with Discloser or to develop competing products or services.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All right, title, and interest in and to any improvements, modifications, derivative works, or other intellectual property developed by Recipient using or based on the Confidential Information shall be assigned and transferred to Discloser upon creation, free of any liens or encumbrances. Recipient hereby grants Discloser a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use any feedback, suggestions, or ideas Recipient may provide regarding the Confidential Information.

5. TERM

The obligations of confidentiality and non-use under this Agreement shall continue in perpetuity and shall not expire, regardless of any termination of discussions between the parties.

6. REMEDIES

Recipient acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to Discloser and that monetary damages would be inadequate. Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief without the need to post a bond, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Cayman Islands, without regard to its conflict of laws provisions. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Cayman Islands for any disputes arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

8. MISCELLANEOUS

This Agreement is binding upon the parties and their respective successors and assigns. Any waiver or modification of this Agreement must be in writing and signed by Discloser. Recipient may not assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Discloser.
